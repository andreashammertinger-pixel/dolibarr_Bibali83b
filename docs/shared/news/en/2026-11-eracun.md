# eRačun and Fiscalisation 2.0 – does it apply to the service charge and the reserve?

<p class="newsmeta">18.11.2026 · eRačun · fiscalisation · taxes</p>

Since **1 January 2026**, VAT-registered businesses in Croatia must issue invoices to each other as **structured eRačun** and fiscalise them; non-VAT businesses follow from **1 January 2027**.

A **community of owners** (*zajednica suvlasnika*) is generally **not liable for VAT or profit tax** and is therefore not an issuer of eRačun. Your service charge (operating costs, reserve) is issued **without VAT and without eRačun**. The manager's own fee to the community is subject to the eRačun rules.
