# New Act on the Management and Maintenance of Buildings (ZUOZ)

<p class="newsmeta">18.11.2026 · ZUOZ · reserve fund · deadlines</p>

Since **1 January 2025** the *Zakon o upravljanju i održavanju zgrada* (ZUOZ, NN 152/24) applies in Croatia.

## Key points

- **Minimum reserve:** 0.54 % of the *standard construction price* published by the Ministry, per m² and year. At €796.34/m² this is about **€4.30/m² per year ≈ €0.36/m² per month**.
- **Annual programme:** the manager proposes a maintenance programme for the following year each autumn. If **no** annual or multi-year programme is adopted, the reserve must be paid at **five times** the last adopted amount.
- **Reports and meeting:** the owners' representative reports on the previous year by **1 February**; owners meet at least **once a year**.

## What does this mean for Bibali 83b?

The reserve rates (House 1: 0.80 · House 2: 0.90 · House 3: €1.00/m² per month) are well **above the minimum** and, according to the reserve plan, cover the needs of the next 20 years. The annual programme is adopted in November/December, followed by the annual service charge; the annual statement follows by the end of February.
