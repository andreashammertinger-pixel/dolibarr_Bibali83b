# Annual statement and reserve fund

## Annual statement

The operating costs in the service charge are **advance payments** based on the budget. After year end (by the end of February) you receive the **statement** with the actual costs:

| Result | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Credit** | automatically offset against your next monthly instalments |
| **Additional payment** | you receive a separate invoice with due date and payment reference |

The statement shows each cost type with the total amount and your share in % and in euros.

## Reserve fund (pričuva)

- Required by law (Zakon o upravljanju i održavanju zgrada).
- **Not settled** – it remains in the reserve account of **your house**.
- Used for maintenance and renewal according to the **reserve plan**; the next planned measures are listed in the explanation.
- The buildings are inspected annually; timing and scope of measures are adjusted to their condition.
