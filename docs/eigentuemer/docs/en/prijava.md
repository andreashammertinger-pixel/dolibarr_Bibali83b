# Sign in

1. Open **[dolibarr.greenresorts.hr](https://dolibarr.greenresorts.hr)** – on a computer or phone.
2. Enter your **login** and **password** (provided by the property manager) → *Login*.

!!! tip "Password forgotten?"
    On the login page click *Password forgotten?* – you will receive an e-mail with a link. If that does not work, write to us: [office@greenresorts.hr](mailto:office@greenresorts.hr).

## Language

Dolibarr shows the language of your browser or the language set for your account. If you would like a different language, let us know – we will set it for you.

## On your phone

Dolibarr works in your phone's browser. Tip: add the page to your home screen (Share → *Add to Home Screen*) to use it like an app.

## Help in Dolibarr

The **Help** menu opens this page directly in your language.
