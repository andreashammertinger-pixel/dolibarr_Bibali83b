# My invoices and documents

In Dolibarr you see **only your own apartment** – other owners cannot see your data.

## Service charges

Menu **Charges | Payment → Service charges**

- list of your service charges with amount and status (*unpaid*, *paid*),
- click an invoice → **documents** at the bottom:

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `IN2611-0001.pdf` | service charge – **Croatian** |
| `IN2611-0001_en_US.pdf` | in your language |
| `IN2611-0001_info….pdf` | **explanation**: how the amount is made up |

Under *Payments* you see which payments have already been recorded.

## Statements and other documents

Menu **Apartments & contacts** → your apartment (e.g. *Bibali 83 B - Apt 1.8*) → tab **Linked files**

Here you find **everything we have sent you** – service charges, explanations, annual statements – each with the sending date at the start of the file name (e.g. `2026-11-18_IN2611-0001.pdf`), plus other documents for your apartment.

!!! note "E-mail"
    You also receive all documents by e-mail. No paper copies are sent.
