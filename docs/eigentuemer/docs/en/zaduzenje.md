# Understanding the service charge

The service charge is issued by the **community of owners** (*Zgrada Bibali 83B*). The property manager is *Green Villa and Apartment Resorts d.o.o.*

## Three items – all based on living area

| Item | What is it? |
|---|---|
| **Operating costs** | running common costs: building insurance, maintenance (treatment plant, roof, air conditioning), stairwell cleaning and electricity, garden, buffer |
| **Reserve fund** (*pričuva*) | legally required reserve of your house for maintenance (roof, facade …) – in a separate account for each house |
| **Management** | property management fee (€0.30/m² + 25 % VAT) |

Each item = **rate in €/m² × living area of your apartment**. Rates differ between houses because costs of a single house (e.g. insurance, garden) are borne by that house only, and the reserve is set according to the needs of the building.

The exact calculation for your apartment is in the **explanation** (PDF with every service charge).

## Paying

- The service charge usually covers a whole year and is paid in **monthly instalments**, each due on the **5th of the month**.
- Easiest: a **standing order** for the monthly instalment.
- **Payment reference** = invoice number (e.g. `IN2611-0001`) – please always state it so that your payment is matched correctly.
- Bank details are on the service charge and in the e-mail.

!!! info "Received late?"
    If you receive a service charge only after the due date, you have **7 days** for that instalment.
